    Associated Places Bhāskararāyapuram (l_11) (connection: Resided) Mahalingeswarar Temple (l_7) (connection: Worshipped) Thirukoteeswarar Temple (l_54) (connection: Visited) Thiruvidaimarudur (l_6) (connection: Died)
    Notes
    'The post-script in the manuscript of the Avaidikadarśanasaṃgraha of Gaṅgādhara Vājapeyin is very important (Tanjore Ms. 8244, Des. Cat. XIV, pp. 6410-12) in that it shows not only the great and well-known friendship between Gaṅgādhara Vājapeyin and Bhāskararāya, but also the relation that the two had with King Śāhaji; the manuscript was copied by Bhāskararāya; and he went out of the way to express at the end of the manuscript his feelings of regard towards Śāhaji with an eulogy of eight verses on him'. (See Raghavan, Venkataraman. Śāhendravilāsa of Śrīdhara Veṅkaṭeśa (A Poem on the Life of King Śāhaji of Tanjore, 1684-1710). Tiruchi: The Kalyan Press, 1952: p. 19).
    He was of an earlier generation to King Śāhaji. (See Raghavan, Venkataraman. Śāhendravilāsa of Śrīdhara Veṅkaṭeśa (A Poem on the Life of King Śāhaji of Tanjore, 1684-1710) Tiruchi: The Kalyan Press, 1952: p. 21).
    One story, recounted (August 2026) by the young priest at the Thirukoteeswarar temple, is that Bhāskararāyamakhin would have written his bhāṣya on the Lalitasahasranāma in that location (others say that he did so at the Madhyarjuna temple).
    References Raghavan, Venkataraman. Śāhendravilāsa of Śrīdhara Veṅkaṭeśa (A Poem on the Life of King Śāhaji of Tanjore, 1684-1710). Tiruchi: The Kalyan Press, 1952.

    Notes
    According to V. Krishnamacharya, Rāmānanda is the name undertaken by Lakṣmaṇa (or Lakṣmīdhara) of the Carakūri family. Before he became a saṃnyāsin with the name Rāmananda, after being married for a long time, under the preceptor Kṛṣṇānandayati (author of the Siddhāntasiddhāñjana), he authored the following: 1) Ṣaḍbhāṣācandrikā, 2) Prasannarāghavavyākhyā: Timmabhūpālīyā, 3) Gītagovindavyākhyā: Srutirañjanī, and 4) Anargharāghavavyākhyā: Iṣṭārthakalpavallī. Rāmānanda mentions these biographical details in the colophon of the Anargharāghavavyākhyā. (See Krishnamacharya, V. ‘Śāstradīpikāvyākhyā: Prabhāmaṇḍala’. In The Adyar Library Bulletin, Vol. 10, pp. 63–68, 1946: p. 65)
    For the name Rāmabhadra Bhagavatpāda, see Raghavan, Venkataraman. Śāhendravilāsa of Śrīdhara Veṅkaṭeśa (A Poem on the Life of King Śāhaji of Tanjore, 1684-1710). Tiruchi: The Kalyan Press, 1952: p. 33.
    He copied Kṛṣṇānanda Sarasvatī's Siddhāntasiddhāñjana in 1670.
    He is different from another Rāmānanda Sarasvatī, the author of a subcommentary on Śaṅkara's Brahmasūtrabhāṣya called Ratnaprabhā and a treatise aligned with the Vivaraṇa interpretation of the Brahmasūtras titled Vivaraṇopanyāsa. See Duquette, Jonathan. Debates in the Delta: An Intellectual History of Late Advaita. Brill, forthcoming.
    It is possible that he was called Rāmayati. See New Catalogus Catalogurum (NCC). Vols 1-36: vol. 24, p. 202b.
    V. Krishnamacharya gives the date that Rāmananda copied the Siddhāntasiddhāñjana as 16th Jan 1670 AD (the 5th day of the bright fortnight in the Makara month of the year Saumya). (See Krishnamacharya, V. ‘Śāstradīpikāvyākhyā: Prabhāmaṇḍala’. In The Adyar Library Bulletin, Vol. 10, pp. 63–68, 1946: p. 67).
